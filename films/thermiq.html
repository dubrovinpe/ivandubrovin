Thermiq

Short Film · Science Fiction · 22 Minutes · 2020

Synopsis

The astronaut Philipp is on a lonely mission to an unknown planet. Isolation and stress amplify his inner conflicts. Suddenly, his deceased mother appears on the space station, bringing old wounds and emotional conflicts to the surface. Philipp must decide whether to stay on the planet or continue his mission. At the same time, the AI Mission Control and mother figure compete for his trust, which increases emotional pressure. The film combines melancholic imagery, contrasts inspired by German Expressionism, and AI-generated planetary landscapes to visually mirror inner conflicts and identity questions.
