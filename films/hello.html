Hello

Short Film · Drama · 9 Minutes · 2023

Synopsis

In a large city in China, the individual is just a dot on a huge wall of other dots. Social media and entrenched notions of masculinity make it difficult for young men to find a serious relationship. Xiao lives here. He is depressed and longs for a girlfriend. One day he invites Lin to his home – but will he manage to open up and overcome his fear of intimacy?
